Doing the work that matters

Progress in local government isn't about flashy announcements; it’s about the steady, sometimes boring work of making our community function better. Over the last term, my focus has been on intentional growth and making sure our city services actually keep pace with our population.

Healthcare
Delivered

Opened a new medical clinic for 10 family doctors

The new Goldstream Medical Clinic, opened in 2026 with local partners, will host 10 family physicians to provide essential primary care for over 12,000 South Island residents this year.
Environment Parks & Recreation Sustainable Development
Delivered

Protected over 200 acres of municipal park land

Langford previously sold off millions of dollars in amenity lands to help keep taxes low during Covid. This new bylaw prevents that practice, permanently protecting 209 acres as public parks for our community’s future.
Public Safety
Delivered

Fixed our critical firefighter shortage

We caught up on a critical staffing shortfall in our fire department by hiring 27 additional firefighters. This means when you call for help, Langford Fire Rescue has the staff ready to respond quickly.

Spearheaded bringing e-bike and e-scooter sharing to the CRD

Beep beep! Have you tried out any of the shared e-bikes and e-scooters yet? More stops are rolling out across the CRD as more neighbours start to see the success of this fun and increasingly convenient transportation option.

Delivered

Built safe routes to school

After years of only budgeting $400,000 to add new sidewalks every year, this term has seen over $15 million spent on road improvements for pedestrians and cyclists alone. Our new Active Transportation Plan will make sure that moving forward, we’re collecting the right amount of roadway and money needed to build safe street infrastructure from the get go.
Parks & Recreation Environment Sustainable Development
Delivered

Secured critical greenspace in downtown Langford

After meeting them while door-knocking in 2022, the owners of Woodlands Park approached me to help them turn their 6.6 acres into a City Park. They didn’t want to see the last significant greenspace in downtown Langford be developed into more condos. In 2030, Woodlands Park will become Langford’s newest downtown oasis.
Environment Sustainable Development
Delivered

Ended installments of artificial grass on boulevards

If you’ve ever wondered why the grass is always greener in Langford, it’s because we used to require it! By turfing the artificial grass requirement, maintenance costs have been reduced for taxpayers until it can be replaced in due time. 
Sustainable Development Governance
Delivered

Ended the misuse of amenity funds

A budget shows a city's real priorities. We phased out the unsustainable use of development funds to keep taxes artificially low, aligning with BC's Bill 46. This caused a one-time 4% tax adjustment in 2024, but it structurally secured the predictable funding we need to build critical infrastructure—like safe sidewalks to schools. 
Sustainable Development
Delivered

Created a new bylaw for peaceful neighbourhoods

After years of rapid growth, construction fatigue is real. We've levelled the playing field for the people of Langford by setting clear rules for construction, ensuring reasonable noise levels, and giving the City new tools to fine nuisance properties and unkept properties in the event of stalled developments.
Environment Sustainable Development
Delivered

Planted thousands of trees

Did you know that our densest, lower-income neighbourhoods currently have the fewest trees? With our new forest plan, tree protections, and a massive planting effort, we are making our city greener and fairer for everyone.

Broke Ground on a new Arts, Culture, and Childcare Centre

Using provincial grants, we are building a new two-storey childcare and arts centre. This mass-timber facility will provide childcare for 49 children plus space for pottery, dance, and community programming.
