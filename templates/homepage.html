The platform

The future is bright

Langford is one of the fastest-growing cities in Canada. That's a gift — and a responsibility. Colby's platform is built to make sure growth works for everyone who calls this city home.

Housing for Everyone

More gentle density, streamlined permitting, and partnerships with non-profits to build homes people can actually afford to live in.

Local Healthcare

Advocate for the Westshore's fair share of Island Health resources and support the expansion of the new medical clinic.

Green & Connected

Expand the trail network, protect green corridors, and push for better transit connections to Victoria and the region.
