The future is bright

My aim is to make Langford more affordable, connected, and fun. I'm focusing on bringing the right people and ideas together to keep us moving forward, together.

  1. 1.1Form partnerships with non-profit organizations to create opportunities to co-locate affordable housing with key services on city-owned land.
  2. 1.2Explore partnerships with non-profit organizations to build family-sized affordable housing using the City’s Affordable Housing Reserve Fund.
  3. 1.3Support OCP-compliant non-market and affordable housing applications through Inclusionary Zoning and priority review processes.
  4. 1.4Support the implementation of new Bylaws for Energy Step Code and Zero Carbon Step Codes to be required in new developments.
  5. 1.5Continue requiring new developments to contribute improved amenities as a requirement of increased density (e.g. car-share, large secure bike storage spaces, public gathering spaces).
  6. 1.6Review the Interface Fire Hazard DPA to ensure higher standards for developments in high-risk areas.
  7. 1.7Investigate opportunities for greater commitments from developers to provide desirable play amenities and leisure spaces for residents.
  8. 1.8Work with the Province to help Langford seniors gain housing security by building age-friendly, subsidized housing with access to community supports.
  9. 1.9Explore the creation of a new Tenant Protection Bylaw with supports for renters and manufactured home park residents facing displacement from development.
  10. 1.10Support development opportunities for essential local services and businesses in existing residential areas as a way to create walkable neighbourhood destinations.
  11. 1.11Seek partnerships with non-profit operators to create affordable housing opportunities within existing residential properties owned by the City of Langford.

  2. 2.2Expedite solutions to address residential parking, speeding and traffic congestion concerns in consultation with local neighbours.
  3. 2.3Hire dedicated bylaw staff to focus on parking enforcement in Langford’s commercial and high-density residential neighbourhoods.
  4. 2.4Advocate for the Province to give Langford a greater voice on transit by getting a seat on the Victoria Regional Transit Commission.
  5. 2.5Work with BC Transit to improve Rapid Service to Downtown Victoria and Frequent transit service levels through Langford’s residential neighbourhoods.
  6. 2.6Fund quick build projects identified in the Active Transportation Plan to support traffic calming and protected infrastructure.
  7. 2.7Support funding free transit passes for Langford youth aged 13–18, funded through parking enforcement fees and taxation.
  8. 2.8Continue working with SD62 to form safe walking, biking and rolling routes for all school areas in Langford.
  9. 2.9Lobby for grants to accelerate the construction of a pedestrian/cyclist bridge over Hwy 1 ahead of the completion of the North Langford Secondary School.
  10. 2.10Continue advocating for the completion of the E&N Trail to Humpback Road to be a regional priority, in partnership with the CRD and Westhills Land Corporation.
  11. 2.11Encourage further expansion of Langford’s shared transportation network through car-share and EV infrastructure into residential areas.
