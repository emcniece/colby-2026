Growing up here

I was born, raised, and educated in Langford. My family came to Vancouver Island in the 1970s, opening a business on Goldstream Avenue. Growing up, I lived in a mobile home park in Goldstream Meadows. When I was eight, my mother fled domestic violence, and we moved into military housing. That same year, my sister was hit by a car while crossing Humpback Road. This accident resulted in lifelong disabilities and taught me early on the importance of family support.

These experiences shaped my perspective on what makes a community thrive. I spent my teenage years renting in Glen Lake, balancing school, sports, and advocacy. After graduating from the old Belmont in 2014, I stayed in Langford, working at Bin 4 while commuting to UVic. My background in environmental outreach and transportation planning gave me a deep understanding of municipal processes. As a caregiver for my grandparents, I also learned the necessity of a city that supports residents at every stage of life. Today, I am a Master's student researching aging adults and equity in transportation.

Serving our community

I ran for Council in 2022 because I saw our city growing quickly, but felt the heart of our community and the needs of families like mine were being left behind. Since being elected, I have focused on intentional growth, public engagement, and transparency. By leading through collaboration, I have helped build partnerships that are delivering tangible improvements to our infrastructure and public services.

There is still work to be done. As a young professional building a life in Langford, I understand the struggle of trying to keep up. My vision is to make Langford more affordable, connected, and fun. In seeking re-election, I am focused on bringing people and ideas together to reduce cost of living pressures and help more residents move forward.

Join me in creating a city we can all be a part of.
